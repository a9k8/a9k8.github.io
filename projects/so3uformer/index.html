SO3UFormer.

Learning Intrinsic Spherical Features for Rotation-Robust Panoramic Dense Prediction — Qinfeng Zhu, Yunxi Jiang and Lei Fan.

Read paper ↗GitHub ↗Publication record ↗

Project premise

Panoramic images live on a sphere. Their representations should respect that geometry.

SO3UFormer studies intrinsic spherical features for panoramic dense prediction, with an explicit focus on robustness to rotation. The project connects the geometry of the observation domain to the learned representation rather than treating a panorama as an ordinary planar image.
